## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-trust, utilitarian, and premium service marketplace tailored to Uzbekistan’s fast-evolving digital economy. The core value proposition—connecting homeowners directly with dependable craftspeople (*ustalar*)—demands an aesthetic of quiet authority, verified safety, and uncompromising clarity.

The visual direction combines **Modern Corporate/SaaS** utility with warm, grounded touches from physical craft:
- **Tone & Mood:** Trustworthy, reliable, pristine, and accessible across mobile bandwidths and varying screen sizes.
- **Target Audience:** Urban and suburban homeowners seeking verified plumbers, electricians, builders, and appliance technicians, alongside independent trade specialists requiring a professional portfolio surface.
- **Linguistic Context:** Built specifically around Uzbek Latin typography (`o‘`, `g‘`, `sh`, `ch`), demanding careful vertical metrics and generous ascender/descender breathing space to prevent clipped glyphs in badges and input fields.
- **Design Metaphor:** "Verified Blueprint"—crisp structural dividing lines, tactile surface cards, and clear status tokens that eliminate ambiguity around pricing, guarantees, and craftsman credentials.

## Colors

The palette balances institutional trustworthiness with operational urgency and safety certifications.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (`#1E40AF` - Royal Indigo):** Anchors navigation, key conversion buttons, high-priority interactive states, and top-tier branding elements. Expresses stability, institutional guarantee, and corporate accountability.
- **Secondary (`#D97706` - Craft Amber):** Evokes tools, construction safety, and active progress. Reserved for urgent actions, request triggers, and highlight ribbons.
- **Tertiary (`#059669` - Verification Emerald):** The foundational trust engine. Used exclusively for verified master badges (*Tasdiqlangan Usta*), official background checks, warranty confirmations, and positive state alerts.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A` - Slate Charcoal):** High-legibility text anchor. Prevents harsh optical contrast while maintaining crisp readability across varying sunlight environments.

### Supporting Canvas & Structural Tones
- **Canvas Base:** `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) delivers clean separation behind white cards.
- **Surface Elevation:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) for actionable cards, modal sheets, and toolbars.
- **Subtle Borders:** `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200) for structural framing and inputs; `#CBD5E1` (Slate 300) for hover states.
- **Star & Review Rating Accent:** `#EAB308` (Golden Amber) strictly isolated to star icons and numeric ratings.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans is selected across all tiers for its modern geometry, friendly aperture curves, and high legibility at micro-sizes. 

### Typographic Principles
- **Uzbek Latin Character Integrity:** Special glyphs such as `o‘`, `g‘`, `sh`, and `ch` require explicit line-height buffers. Never set `line-height` below `1.2x` on large titles or below `1.4x` on body copy to ensure diacritics and apostrophe marks do not collide with adjacent lines.
- **Numbers & Rates:** Prices (e.g., *150,000 so‘m/soat*) must be set using tabular numeric properties (`tnum`) in tables and craftsman bid comparisons to maintain horizontal alignment.
- **Microcopy Hierarchy:** Badges, trust pills, and ratings leverage `label-sm` with slight tracking (`0.04em`) and uppercase transformation where category clarity is essential.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an **8-point mathematical grid** (with a secondary 4px baseline step for micro-elements) inside a structured responsive frame.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Grids
- **Mobile (< 640px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` (16px) margins and `1rem` gutters. Search, booking requests, and craftsman action bars stick permanently to the bottom thumb zone.
- **Tablet (640px – 1024px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` (32px) margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Service filters transition into an off-canvas drawer.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout capped at `1280px` maximum content width, centered with `3rem` gutters. Split layout surfaces display filter controls on the left (4 columns) and searchable profile cards on the right (8 columns).

### Spacing Usage Rules
- `space-xs` (4px): Icon-to-text spacing, rating count badges, and indicator dots.
- `space-sm` (8px): Internal form label-to-input gaps, chip-to-chip inline spacing.
- `space-md` (16px): Standard internal padding for cards, input field content padding, and list item separators.
- `space-lg` (24px): Outer spacing between distinct card sections, modal padding, and grouped metadata blocks.
- `space-xl` (40px): Vertical section rhythm between service category blocks and landing page segments.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy, dark drop shadows. Depth is achieved via **low-contrast outlines** paired with **ambient, blue-tinted diffuse shadows** that feel airy and clean under daylight conditions.

### Depth Tiers
- **Flat (Level 0):** Background surface (`#F8FAFC`). No elevation, unbordered.
- **Structured (Level 1 - Cards, List Rows):** `#FFFFFF` background with a crisp border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Raised (Level 2 - Hovered Craftsman Cards, Active Filters):** Border shifts to `1px solid #CBD5E1`. Shadow: `0 8px 16px -4px rgba(30, 64, 175, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. The primary blue tint indicates interactivity.
- **Floating (Level 3 - Sticky Bottom Action Bar, Filter Popovers):** Border `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Shadow: `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 4px 8px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.
- **Overlay (Level 4 - Modals, Direct Booking Drawers):** Shadow: `0 25px 50px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.25)`. Combined with a backdrop blur of `4px` over `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Rounded (`2`)** geometric profile. This strikes an intentional balance: approachable enough for everyday home services, yet structured enough to preserve professional authority.

### Radius Specifications
- **Base Components (Inputs, Small Badges, Inline Chips):** `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Cards, Service Tiles, Modals, Action Panels:** `1rem` (16px) via `rounded-lg`.
- **Full Enclosures (Avatar Frames, Floating Filter Buttons, Status Indicators):** `9999px` (Pill) strictly for verification markers and user avatars.
- **Decorative Media (Before/After Craftsmanship Portfolios):** Must follow the parent card's `rounded-lg` radius on outer edges, with `0.5rem` inner thumbnail rounding.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** `#1E40AF` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` border radius. Height `48px` (desktop), `52px` (mobile touch target). Subtle active press compression (`scale(0.99)`).
- **Secondary (Craft Action):** `#D97706` background, `#FFFFFF` text. Used for emergency calls (*Shoshilinch chaqiruv*) and immediate quotation requests.
- **Outline:** Transparent background, `1.5px solid #E2E8F0`, `#0F172A` text. Hover shifts to `#F8FAFC` background with `#1E40AF` border and text.

### Verification Badge (*Tasdiqlangan Usta*)
- Distinctive component: `#ECFDF5` background, `1px solid #A7F3D0` border, `#047857` label text with an embedded checkmark shield icon in `#059669`. Displayed on profiles, bid sheets, and search result headers.

### Craftsman Cards (*Usta Kartochkasi*)
- Clean white surface with `1px solid #E2E8F0` and Level 1 elevation.
- Structure: Top row features usta avatar (64px with green verification dot), full name, trade category chip, and overall rating (`★ 4.9 (128 ta fikr)`). Middle block features price estimate (*Narx: kelishilgan holda* or hourly rate) and serviced city districts (*Toshkent, Mirzo Ulug‘bek tumani*). Bottom row provides secondary messaging action and direct call button.

### Inputs & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, height `48px`, border `1.5px solid #E2E8F0`, padding `0 16px`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#1E40AF` with a subtle focus ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(30, 64, 175, 0.15)`).
- Phone number input: Fixed `+998` prefix container separated by a vertical `#E2E8F0` divider line.

### Category Chips
- Pill or `0.5rem` rounded badges. Unselected: `#F1F5F9` background, `#475569` text. Selected: `#1E40AF` background, `#FFFFFF` text. Icon prefix (e.g., wrench, lightning bolt, paintbrush) scaled to `16px`.

### Rating Displays
- Gold star (`#EAB308`) filled glyph, followed by bold numeric score (`4.95`), paired with slate muted count in parentheses.
